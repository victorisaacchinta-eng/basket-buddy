The problem, with real numbers

Quick-commerce apps sell the same groceries at different prices, and comparing them today means opening three or four apps by hand.

What shoppers deal with today

  • ✕ Open Blinkit, check price, close it
  • ✕ Open Zepto, check again, close it
  • ✕ Open Amazon, one more time
  • ✕ Existing comparison tools use cached, scraped indexes that go stale the moment a price changes

What Basket Buddy does instead

  • ✓ One live, authenticated session per platform
  • ✓ Prices pulled at the moment you ask, not cached
  • ✓ Remembers each platform's layout, gets faster next time
  • ✓ Read-only by design, no checkout, no payment risk

Three ways to look at it

SHOPPER

Never re-check by hand

One list, checked everywhere at once, at the actual live price, not a stale index.

PLATFORM

Wins the order, or learns why not

Blinkit is owned by Eternal, already the largest quick-commerce player. If Basket Buddy checks and Blinkit wins, the order completes right there. If it loses, Blinkit sees exactly which item and margin it lost.

JUDGE

Provable, not asserted
